# 1. Introduction

This document defines the complete 3D world used by the portfolio.

Unlike a traditional website, the portfolio exists inside a continuous virtual
environment.

Every section belongs to one connected world.

The user never changes pages.

The camera simply travels through different areas of the world.

---

# 2. World Vision

The world represents the digital mind of an AI Engineer.

Think of it as an advanced AI operating system that has become a physical space.

The visitor enters the system, explores its knowledge, examines its work, and
finally reaches the communication terminal.

Everything should feel engineered.

Nothing should feel decorative.

---

# 3. World Philosophy

The world follows five principles.

---

## Infinite

The world should never appear to end.

Backgrounds fade naturally into fog and space.

---

## Connected

Every section physically connects to the next.

No disconnected scenes.

---

## Layered

Foreground

↓

Content

↓

Architecture

↓

Environment

↓

Infinite Space

---

## Living

Nothing remains perfectly still.

Small movement exists everywhere.

---

## Minimal

Avoid visual noise.

Large empty spaces improve focus.
